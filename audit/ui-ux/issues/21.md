## Problem

Below `lg`, the Matrix and its issue detail stack with the matrix first. Tapping a cell updates the URL and the cell's thin selection bar, but the section that names the quadrant and lists its issues starts below the fold (heading top ≈904 on an 844px viewport, `scrollY = 0`). The user sees no content change. The matrix table is 480px wide inside a 364px area at 390px, so the Low urgency column is off-screen with no scroll cue.

## Affected routes

- `/matrix` at 390×844 (light and dark).

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/matrix` at 390×844.
2. Tap the Moderate/Mid cell.
3. Observe the viewport and the detail heading position.

## Actual behavior

Only the cell border changes; the inspector heading is off-screen. The Low column is outside the visible table area.

## Expected behavior

Selecting a cell produces an immediately visible result — the detail/summary adjacent to or above the matrix, or the page scrolls/focuses the detail heading. All three urgency columns are reachable with an obvious horizontal-scroll affordance if needed.

## Impact

On the required mobile viewport, cell selection appears to do nothing. Users cannot follow the select → inspect workflow without guessing to scroll, and some quadrants are undiscoverable.

## Suggested direction

At `<lg`, place the selected-cell summary directly under the grid or render the detail as an expandable section beneath the selected row, and move focus to the detail heading after selection. Reduce grid padding so all three columns fit at 390px, or add an explicit scroll cue.
