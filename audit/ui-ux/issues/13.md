## Problem

The Epics list, epic child table, and Milestones list keep every desktop column at every width and rely on `.table-responsive` horizontal scrolling. Nothing hides low-priority columns, moves values into a metadata line, or signals that content is off-screen.

Measured:

- `/epics` at 768px: 1188px table in a 742px region; Status, Progress, Estimate, Actual and Delay are hidden.
- `/epics` at 390px: 1198px table in 364px; the title is clipped mid-word.
- `/epics/.../10` at 768px: 1127px child table; status is cut to "In prog"/"In revie", and the actions column is unreachable.
- `/milestones` at 390px: 716px table in 364px; due date is clipped ("2026-12-1("), and Progress is entirely off-screen.

## Affected routes

- `/epics`, `/epics/[owner]/[repo]/[number]`, `/milestones` at 768×1024 and 390×844.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/epics` at 768×1024 or 390×844.
2. Observe which columns are visible and whether the title is clipped.
3. Repeat on `/milestones` and an epic detail page.

## Actual behavior

Primary values (status, progress, due date) are off-screen or clipped, with no affordance indicating horizontal scroll. Clipped values look like broken data.

## Expected behavior

At `md` and below, keep a readable subset (title + status/progress/due as a metadata line) and avoid horizontal scrolling for the primary content. The epic child table hides time columns or stacks them under the title and keeps the remove action reachable.

## Impact

The organization views are effectively desktop-only. Tablet and phone users cannot see an epic's progress or a milestone's due date without discovering an unmarked scroll area.

## Suggested direction

Apply the responsive column strategy used by the issue index: hide secondary columns below `md`/`lg` and render their values in a compact metadata line. For the child table, drop Estimate/Actual/Delay below `md` and keep actions reachable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `/epics` at 390px and 768px shows title, status and progress without horizontal scrolling.
- [ ] `/milestones` at 390px shows due date and progress without clipping.
- [ ] The epic child table at 768px shows full status text and a reachable remove action.
- [ ] No table cell text is clipped mid-word at the right edge.

**Priority:** P2 — organization views are unusable on required tablet/mobile viewports.
