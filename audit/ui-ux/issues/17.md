## Problem

Milestone due dates always render as plain `YYYY-MM-DD` text. A past-due milestone with open work is visually identical to one months in the future: no overdue word, icon or colour on either the list or detail page. The Timeline already computes `isOverdue` (due date in the past and progress < 1); the milestone views do not use it.

## Affected routes

- `/milestones` and `/milestones/[owner]/[repo]/[number]`.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Set a milestone's due date to a past date with incomplete work.
2. Open `/milestones` and the milestone detail.
3. Compare the past-due row with future-dated rows.

## Actual behavior

All dates are styled identically. A missed date carries no signal.

## Expected behavior

A past due date with incomplete work is labelled "Overdue" using text plus a Bootstrap icon (danger tone), consistent with the Timeline. A completed milestone with a past date is not marked overdue.

## Impact

The milestone list is where schedule risk should be visible, yet a missed date is indistinguishable from a healthy one. Users must compare each ISO date against today mentally.

## Suggested direction

Reuse the Timeline's `isOverdue` calculation. Render `Overdue · 2026-10-20` in danger text with an icon when due and incomplete; keep the plain date otherwise. Apply the same rule in the detail meta line.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A past-due, incomplete milestone is visually distinct on list and detail.
- [ ] The treatment uses text and an icon, not colour alone.
- [ ] A completed milestone with a past due date is not marked overdue.
- [ ] Wording matches the Timeline's "Overdue" label.

**Priority:** P2 — schedule risk is invisible.
