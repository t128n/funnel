## Problem

Error and not-found surfaces are inconsistent and often dead ends:

- An unknown URL renders the root error page outside the app shell: no navbar, theme control or skip link. It shows "Page not found" then "Not Found", and the primary **Try Again** re-requests the same missing URL.
- A missing issue renders an unstyled red line "Issue demo/funnel#999 is not mirrored", with Retry at the far right, "Return to Issues" as a plain link, no `h1`, and an "Open in GitHub" button pointing to a non-existent issue.
- Missing milestone/epic routes also drop the shell, say "Milestone is not mirrored", and offer "Back to Issues" rather than the matching list.

## Affected routes

- `/nonexistent-route`, `/issues/[owner]/[repo]/[number]` (missing), `/milestones/.../999`, `/epics/.../999`, `/inbox/submission/<missing>`.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/nonexistent-route` and click Try Again.
2. Open `/issues/demo/funnel/999` and inspect the message and GitHub button.
3. Open `/milestones/demo/funnel/999`.

## Actual behavior

The error surface loses navigation, repeats the status, exposes internal sync vocabulary ("mirrored"), and its most prominent action cannot succeed. Recovery links point to the wrong section.

## Expected behavior

Errors keep the shell where possible, use plain language ("This issue does not exist or is not available"), group recovery actions around the relevant list, and only offer Try Again for retryable 5xx/network failures. Missing entities do not offer "Open in GitHub".

## Impact

A mistyped or stale URL strands the user outside the app's navigation and suggests a sync fault where the real answer is "this does not exist". Dead actions waste the recovery step.

## Suggested direction

Render 404s inside the app shell (or a focused surface with section-appropriate recovery), map messages per entity, hide actions that cannot work, and reserve reload for retryable failures. Fix the duplicated status copy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A 404 on an app route keeps the navbar, theme and skip link where possible.
- [ ] Copy avoids internal sync terms and states the status once.
- [ ] Recovery actions point to the relevant list (Issues, Milestones, Epics, Inbox).
- [ ] Try Again is absent for non-recoverable 4xx.
- [ ] Missing issues do not offer Open in GitHub.

**Priority:** P2 — errors lose navigation and offer dead-end recovery.
