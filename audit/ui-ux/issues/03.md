## Problem

At the 768px tablet breakpoint the issue index keeps all six metadata columns plus `Planned`. The fixed widths consume about 640px of the 672px content area, leaving the Title column roughly 102px wide. Titles wrap to one or two words per line, producing 143–239px rows; the page's primary scanning task becomes effectively impossible.

## Affected routes

- `/issues` at 768×1024 (light and dark), with knock-on density at 820–991px.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Set the viewport to 768×1024.
2. Open `/issues`.
3. Measure the title column and row heights, or read the Backlog group.

## Actual behavior

Measured column widths: Issue 112px, Title 102px, Status 128px, Priority 112px, Planned 128px, Assignee 160px. Row heights are 143–239px; a normal title renders over ten lines.

## Expected behavior

At `md`, the title remains the dominant column. Secondary columns are hidden or the table scrolls without compressing Title. Composition A §12 expects ID/title/status/priority/assignee at this width, not a squeezed title plus a mostly empty `Planned` column.

## Impact

Every issue title is hard to read at a required audit viewport, and a handful of issues fills several screens. This is the main browse surface.

## Suggested direction

Hide or collapse `Planned` below `lg`; give the remaining columns explicit small widths and let Title take the remainder. Verify 768, 820 and 991px. If all columns must stay, use the bordered `.table-responsive` scroll instead of wrapping titles.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At 768×1024, the Title column is at least 300px wide in the default view.
- [ ] A normal row does not exceed two title lines.
- [ ] `Planned` is hidden below `lg`, or the table scrolls without compressing Title.
- [ ] No horizontal page overflow at 390px and no regression at 1440px.

**Priority:** P1 — the primary list is unusable at tablet width.
