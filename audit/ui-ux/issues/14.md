## Problem

The Notifications list fails in two ways at its edges:

1. **Mobile titles truncate to a few characters.** At 390px the title shares a flex row with the relative time and "Mark read"; both are `flex-shrink-0`, so titles collapse to about 140px: "Priya comme…", "You were ass…", "New intake:…". Opening the row is the only way to read it, and opening marks it read.
2. **The list silently stops at 100 rows.** The loader requests `limit: 100` while the header counts every unread row. With 110 unread notifications, the header said "110 unread", the bell said "99+", and exactly 100 rows rendered with no pager or total. Ten counted items were unreachable.

## Affected routes

- `/notifications` at 390×844 and at scale on desktop.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/notifications` at 390×844 and read the unread titles.
2. Insert or accumulate more than 100 notifications, then compare the header count with the number of rows and look for pagination.

## Actual behavior

Mobile titles are unreadable without opening each item. The count and the rendered list disagree above 100 items, and older unread items cannot be reached individually.

## Expected behavior

At 390px the title wraps or owns its own line, with time and Mark read beneath it. Every notification included in the unread count is reachable, or the page states "Showing 100 of N" with a way to continue.

## Impact

Phone users cannot scan the feed, and long-lived accounts can have unread items silently disappear from the only notification view while the badge insists they exist.

## Suggested direction

At `xs`, stack the row: title first (wrap to two lines), then time and read action. Add pagination or a "Load older" control driven by the existing count, and show a result total when capped.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At 390px, every notification title is readable in full or wraps to two lines.
- [ ] Time and Mark read no longer compete with the title for horizontal space.
- [ ] Every notification included in the unread count can be reached.
- [ ] The list shows a total or "showing N of M" when capped.
- [ ] Desktop single-line layout is otherwise unchanged.

**Priority:** P2 — unread items become unreadable and unreachable.
