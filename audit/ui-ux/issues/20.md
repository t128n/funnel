## Problem

Timeline label rows are fixed at 44px at 768px and above, but their content is a title plus a second line that wraps to ref, due date and progress. When the second line wraps, the row needs 57–71px. `overflow: hidden` clips the title, and centring pushes it above the row into the sticky header. The first milestone title ("v2.0 — cockpit") is half-cut and labels appear interleaved with the following row. Every row reports `scrollHeight > clientHeight`. At 390px the stylesheet raises the row to 4.5rem, so the defect is specific to desktop/tablet.

## Affected routes

- `/timeline` at 1440×900 and 768×1024, light and dark.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/timeline` with all repositories at 1440×900.
2. Inspect the first milestone row in the Milestone labels column.
3. Resize to 768×1024 and repeat.

## Actual behavior

The first title is clipped by the sticky header; label content overlaps adjacent rows; label rows no longer align one-to-one with chart bands.

## Expected behavior

Each label row is tall enough for its content, or the metadata is truncated to one line. Label rows align with chart bands without clipping at all required widths.

## Impact

The primary textual key for the chart is unreadable for the first milestone and collides with neighbouring rows, so bars cannot be reliably matched to milestones.

## Suggested direction

Let label content drive row height (measure with `min-height`) or constrain the metadata line to one line with `text-truncate`. Keep the chart band height in sync through the existing `measure()` path. Consider widening the label column at `md+`.
