## Problem

Settings validation fails in three compounding ways:

1. The error alert is rendered at the top of the form while Save is at the bottom. After submitting from the bottom of a long page, the alert is injected hundreds of pixels above the viewport, so the click appears to do nothing. On `/settings/planning`, after Save the page stayed at `scrollY = 744` while the alert top was `-586px`.
2. The summary repeats "Settings could not be saved" as both heading and message, then prints raw server keys such as `session.idle_days` and `planning.work_defaults.work_days`.
3. The offending field is not marked invalid, not focused, and has no `aria-describedby`, so the user must translate internal keys back to labels.

## Affected routes

- `/settings/planning`, `/settings/availability`, `/settings/session`, `/settings/repositories`, and all `SettingsForm` pages.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/settings/planning` at 900px height.
2. Uncheck all work days and click Save Changes at the bottom.
3. Observe that nothing visible happens; inspect the alert position.
4. Open `/settings/session`, clear Idle lifetime, save, and inspect the alert and input.

## Actual behavior

- Validation error is off-screen after a bottom-of-form submit.
- The same sentence appears twice, followed by dotted internal keys.
- Inputs keep normal borders and are not focused or described by the error.

## Expected behavior

A failed save scrolls/focuses a single human-readable summary (or shows feedback next to Save), marks the first invalid field with `.is-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, and uses visible labels ("Idle lifetime must be a whole number"). Internal keys stay in logs.

## Impact

Users can repeatedly press Save without realising validation failed, and administrators see implementation vocabulary instead of actionable field guidance.

## Suggested direction

After a failed action, scroll and focus the alert or first invalid field. Deduplicate the summary. Map validator issue paths to field labels and render field-level errors through the settings field wrapper. Keep `role="alert"`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An invalid long-form submit makes the error visible without manual scrolling.
- [ ] Focus lands on the error summary or the first invalid field.
- [ ] The alert contains one summary plus field-specific, human-readable messages; no dotted config keys.
- [ ] Invalid fields carry `.is-invalid` and `aria-describedby`.
- [ ] Behavior is the same for `SettingsForm` pages and the Availability form.

**Priority:** P2 — validation failures appear to do nothing and leak internal keys.
