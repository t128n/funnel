## Problem

The first Tab stop on every app route is "Skip to main content". Its element is focusable and its box is at `(8, 8)`, but it is painted underneath the navbar: `elementsFromPoint` at that location returns `.navbar-brand` and the navbar, not the skip link. The screenshot while the link is focused shows only the normal navbar, with no visible focus indicator.

## Affected routes

- Every route rendered by the app shell.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/inbox` at 1440×900.
2. Press Tab once.
3. Inspect `document.activeElement` and `document.elementsFromPoint(28, 28)`.
4. Take a screenshot while the link is focused.

## Actual behavior

The link receives focus but is invisible behind the navbar. Pressing Enter jumps to `#main`, but a sighted keyboard user sees no focus ring and cannot discover the control.

## Expected behavior

On focus, the skip link is painted above the navbar, fully visible, with a clear focus outline and readable contrast at 1440px and 390px.

## Impact

The primary keyboard bypass for the six-link navbar is effectively invisible on every route.

## Suggested direction

Give the skip link a stacking context above the navbar (or place it after the navbar and use `position: fixed`). Verify with a screenshot and hit-test at both required viewports.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tabbing once reveals a clearly visible skip link over the navbar.
- [ ] The link is the topmost hit target at its own coordinates.
- [ ] Activating it still moves focus to `#main`.
- [ ] Works in light and dark themes and at 390px.

**Priority:** P2 — the keyboard bypass mechanism is unusable.
