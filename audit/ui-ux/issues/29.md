## Problem

After Auto-plan places FUN-4 at 09:00–13:00, the Candidates panel still lists FUN-4 with an enabled **Add** button and the stats still say "1 candidate". Clicking Add attempts a second block at 09:00 and fails with "Blocks overlap at 09:00" plus a **Retry** link. Retrying the same deterministic overlap can never succeed, and the candidate has no "planned" state.

## Affected routes

- `/day`.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/day` with a sized candidate.
2. Click Auto-plan.
3. Click the candidate's Add button again.
4. Observe the overlap error and Retry action.

## Actual behavior

A planned candidate still looks unplanned and can be re-added. The resulting validation error offers a Retry that repeats the same failing request.

## Expected behavior

A planned candidate is marked as scheduled, or Add is disabled/replaced with "Add another block" if splitting is supported. Overlap errors explain how to resolve the conflict and do not offer Retry.

## Impact

Users can trap themselves in a failing action loop and may conclude the day planner is broken. Candidate state contradicts the timeline.

## Suggested direction

Track which candidates already have blocks and render a "Planned" label or disabled Add, or place the block at the next free slot. For overlap errors, offer "Remove overlapping block" or "Clear day"; reserve Retry for transport/server failures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A planned candidate is visibly marked and cannot create a duplicate overlapping block by default.
- [ ] Add places at a free time if splitting is allowed.
- [ ] Overlap errors do not offer Retry; they offer a resolvable action.
- [ ] Candidate state matches the timeline after auto-plan and manual add.

**Priority:** P3 — localized feedback loop in the day planner.
