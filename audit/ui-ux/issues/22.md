## Problem

Several text and control combinations fail contrast in one or both themes:

- In dark mode, `btn-outline-secondary` uses Bootstrap's fixed grey `#6c757d` on `#212529` (3.29:1). Enabled buttons such as "Manage Installation", "Sign out", "Add repository" and "Cancel" look disabled.
- Dark-mode `.text-success` (`#198754`, 3.40:1) and `.text-danger` (`#dc3545`, 3.41:1) fail the 4.5:1 requirement for normal text. "Installed", "Staged for triage", "Saved." and error text are hard to read.
- In light mode, the composer's "Ctrl Enter" hint renders as a dark block with invisible text: background `rgb(33,37,41)`, colour `rgba(33,37,41,.75)`. It reads as an empty or disabled button.

## Affected routes

- `/settings/*` and public success/error states in dark mode; issue detail comment composer in light mode.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Choose Dark from the navbar Theme menu.
2. Open `/settings/github-app` and inspect a secondary outline button and a `.text-success`/`.text-danger` element.
3. Choose Light, open an issue detail, focus the comment box, and inspect the "Ctrl Enter" `<kbd>`.

## Actual behavior

Measured ratios are 3.29:1, 3.40:1 and 3.41:1; the composer hint is effectively 1:1.

## Expected behavior

Enabled secondary controls and semantic text reach at least 4.5:1 against their background in both themes while remaining visually secondary. The shortcut hint is legible and does not look like a button.

## Impact

Dark-mode actions can be mistaken for disabled, success/error states are hard to perceive, and the composer shortcut is undiscoverable.

## Suggested direction

Use colour-mode-aware values for outline-secondary and `--bs-success-text-emphasis`/`--bs-danger-text-emphasis`, or explicit accessible values derived from Bootstrap tokens. For `<kbd>`, keep Bootstrap's default light-on-dark or use a bordered light badge in light mode. Verify with a contrast checker.
