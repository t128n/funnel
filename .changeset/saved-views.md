Add named, shareable saved views for the Issues and Inbox streams. The new **Views** toolbar switcher saves the current filter query, sort, direction and grouping as a preset; choosing one opens the stored URL and restores its grouping. Views can be personal or shared instance-wide, pinned, refreshed from the current filters, renamed and deleted. The URL remains the source of truth and later filter edits are not auto-saved; read-only users can open views but cannot create or change them.
